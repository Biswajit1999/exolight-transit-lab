# Transit exposure and dilution sensitivity (conditional numerical study)

Run from the research directory: `python -m unittest -v test_transit_sensitivity.py`.

Three independent diagnostics: (1) exact top-hat exposure convolution of a box transit, (2) third-light dilution by contaminant/target flux ratio, (3) uniform-disc small-planet radius-ratio and independent first-order uncertainty propagation. The box approximation deliberately excludes ingress/egress, limb darkening, finite integration across changing geometry, spots and correlated errors. It must not replace the existing production worker's transit physics or be interpreted as survey completeness or a new exoplanet measurement.
